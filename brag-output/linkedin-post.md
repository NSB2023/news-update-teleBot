I wanted a better way to catch up with the world on my way to work, so I built MorningBird: a personal audio newspaper for Mac.

You choose the regions, topics and publishers. MorningBird gathers articles through RSS, uses Qwen on your Mac to select and explain the stories, turns the briefing into speech with Pocket TTS, and sends the audio to Telegram.

The news sources and Telegram need an internet connection; the analysis and speech generation run locally. The newspaper-style setup screen lets you shape your own edition.

Here is a 15-second look at the idea. The video shows the interface and an illustrative Telegram delivery; the repository has the working pipeline. I would love feedback on what would make the setup simpler and the briefing more useful for a daily commute.
